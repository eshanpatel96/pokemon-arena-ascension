How to read status

SOURCE: implemented in inspected source, but not gameplay-verified.
PARTIAL: a prototype or subset exists; acceptance needs more implementation.
MISSING: requested behavior is absent or still a placeholder in inspected source.
UNVERIFIED: insufficient evidence to establish implementation or actual behavior.
LATER PLAN: retained campaign/optional feature currently staged beyond V9 by documents; exact feature-level user-approved deferral is not established unless a decision is cited.
SUPERSEDED TARGET: older specification differs from newer direction; preserve the history and verify any material user choice.

A checkmark may be added only with source/build version, test steps, result and evidence. “Compiles,” “sprite exists” and “move profile exists” are not gameplay passes.
